Three rate benchmarks — what each means for Trinity:
GoC 10Y — institutional benchmark. Spread vs cap rate shows if retail is cheap or expensive vs bonds.
GoC 5Y — most Canadian commercial mortgages price off this. Directly affects acquisition financing.
Prime Rate — floating construction loans price off Prime. With Trinity developing 2.2M sq ft, this hits project budgets directly.
